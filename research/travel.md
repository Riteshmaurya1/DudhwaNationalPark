# TRAVEL LOGISTICS & ACCESS GUIDE
## Dudhwa National Park, Lakhimpur Kheri, UP

### 1. Geographical Location & Coordinates
- **District**: Lakhimpur Kheri & Bahraich (Uttar Pradesh)
- **Coordinates**: 28°18′ N to 28°42′ N latitude, 80°28′ E to 80°57′ E longitude
- **Elevation**: 150m – 183m above mean sea level
- **Border**: Northern perimeter lies directly along the international border with Nepal (Mohana River boundary).

---

### 2. Modes of Transit

#### Air Connectivity
- **Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport (Lucknow - LKO)**: ~238 km (4.5–5 hours drive via NH30 and Lakhimpur-Palia highway).
- **Bareilly Airport (BEK)**: ~175 km (3.5–4 hours drive).

#### Rail Transit
- **Palia Kalan (LKU/PLK)**: ~10 km from park entrance (Narrow/Broad gauge connection).
- **Mailani Junction (MLN)**: ~45 km (Major junction connecting to Lucknow and Bareilly).
- **Shahjahanpur (SPN)**: ~107 km (Major mainline station on Delhi-Howrah rail trunk).

#### Road Routes from Major Hubs
- **From Delhi (~430 km, 8-9 hours)**: Delhi → Moradabad → Bareilly → Shahjahanpur → Khutar → Mailani → Palia Kalan → Dudhwa.
- **From Lucknow (~238 km, 4.5 hours)**: Lucknow → Sitapur → Lakhimpur → Gola Gokarannath → Palia Kalan → Dudhwa.

---

### 3. Seasonal Climate Profile & Best Time to Visit

- **Best Time**: **November to April** (Pleasant winter weather, clear wildlife sightings in grasslands).
- **Winter (Nov - Feb)**: Temperatures 4°C – 20°C. Heavy morning fog in phantas; migratory birds active in Jhadi Tal.
- **Summer (Mar - Jun)**: Temperatures 20°C – 40°C. Tigers frequent waterholes along Suheli river.
- **Monsoon (Jun 16 - Nov 14)**: Park is **closed** due to heavy rain and river overflow (Suheli and Mohana).
