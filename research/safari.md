# SAFARI ZONES, TIMINGS, GATE RULES & REGULATIONS
## Dudhwa National Park & Tiger Reserve

### 1. Safari Zones & Gate Architecture

| Zone / Sanctuary | Primary Entrance Gate | Key Wildlife Highlights | Terrain Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhwa Core (Jirauli)** | Jirauli Gate (near Palia Kalan) | Tiger, Rhino RRA, Barasingha, Sal Canopy | Dense Moist Deciduous Sal Forest & Phantas |
| **Sonaripur / Sathiana** | Sonaripur Gate | Kakraha Rhino Area, Barasingha herds | Marshy grasslands, river floodplains |
| **Kishanpur Zone** | Kataiya Gate / Mailani | High Tiger density, Jhadi Tal migratory birds | Wetland lagoon, teak and sal mixture |
| **Katarniaghat Zone** | Bichhia Gate | Gharial, Gangetic Dolphin, Boating, Elephants | Riverine ecosystem along Girwa River |

---

### 2. Operational Seasons & Daily Timings

- **Open Season**: November 15 to June 15 (Closed during Monsoon: June 16 to November 14).
- **Shift 1 (Morning Safari)**: 06:00 AM – 10:00 AM (Winter shift starts 06:30 AM).
- **Shift 2 (Evening Safari)**: 03:00 PM – 06:00 PM (Summer shift extends to 06:30 PM).
- **Elephant Safari**: Subject to forest department availability (primarily for Rhino RRA viewing in South Sonaripur).

---

### 3. Visitor Permits & Tariff Rules

- **Gypsy Allocation**: Registered 4x4 open-top safari Gypsies with accredited forest department drivers and compulsory registered nature guides.
- **Capacity**: Maximum 6 visitors + 1 driver + 1 guide per vehicle.
- **Permit Acquisition**: Mandatory online pre-registration via UP Ecotourism portal (`upecotourism.in`).
- **Code of Conduct**:
  - Plastic-free zone strictly enforced.
  - Zero noise / speed limit 20 km/h inside core forest routes.
  - No disembarking from vehicle outside designated watchtowers (e.g. Bankatti, Salarhapur, Jhadi Tal).
