# SOURCE CONFLICTS & DISCREPANCY RESOLUTION MATRIX
## Dudhwa National Park & Tiger Reserve

### Conflict Records & Authoritative Resolutions

| Discrepancy Topic | Source A Claim | Source B Claim | Root Cause Analysis | Standardized Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Park Area** | Wikipedia: `490.3 sq km` | UP Ecotourism Portal: `680 sq km` | Wikipedia quotes strict core area (`490 km²`), whereas UP Ecotourism includes core plus buffer envelope (`680 km²`). | State both numbers clearly: **Core Area**: 490 km², **Total Enclosure**: 680 km². |
| **Tiger Reserve Area** | Tourism blogs: `1280 sq km` | WII / NTCA Official Audit: `2201.77 sq km` | 1,280 km² represents core sanctuary area; 2,201.77 km² represents total landscape including buffer ranges across Lakhimpur & Bahraich. | Standardize to **Core Reserve**: 1,280 km², **Total Landscape**: 2,201.77 km². |
| **Rhino Population Count** | Older articles (2018): `32 rhinos` | 2022 Forest Dept Survey: `42+ rhinos` | Population growth due to successful breeding in Kakraha RRA over recent years. | Use **42–48 individuals (2022–2026 census)** with explicit review date. |
| **Park Establishment Year** | Popular travel sites: `1987` | Govt NIC Gazette: `1977` | 1977 is the official National Park declaration year. 1987 is when it became a Project Tiger Reserve. | Clarify timeline: **National Park** (1977), **Tiger Reserve** (1987). |

---

### Authoritative Hierarchy Protocol
When conflicting figures are encountered:
1. Defer strictly to official Gazette / WII / UP Forest Dept reports over travel blog aggregators.
2. Store both values in `source-conflicts.md` with explicit notes explaining why the discrepancy exists.
3. Provide hover tooltips in data-heavy UI components explaining core vs. buffer definitions when displaying area metrics.
