# ACCOMMODATION MATRIX — FOREST REST HOUSES & ECO-LODGES
## Dudhwa National Park & Surrounds

### 1. Government Forest Rest Houses (FRHs)
Historic colonial and post-independence British-style forest rest houses situated inside core and buffer forest ranges:

| Property Name | Location / Range | Capacity | Character & Atmosphere | Booking Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhwa FRH** | Main Park Headquarters | 10+ Rooms / Dormitories | Historic colonial building surrounded by deep Sal trees | UP Forest Dept / UP Ecotourism |
| **Sathiana FRH** | Sathiana Range | 4 Suites | Secluded heritage rest house overlooking vast Barasingha phantas | UP Forest Dept (Special Permit) |
| **Sonaripur FRH** | South Sonaripur Range | 4 Rooms | Adjacent to the Kakraha Rhino Rehabilitation enclosure | UP Forest Dept |
| **Bankatti FRH** | Bankatti Range | 2 Suites | Remote forest post near Suheli River | UP Forest Dept |
| **Salarhapur FRH** | Salarhapur Range | 2 Suites | Buffer zone rest house with birdwatching towers | UP Forest Dept |
| **Katarniaghat FRH** | Bichhia, Katarniaghat | 6 Rooms | Perched along the banks of the Girwa river | UP Forest Dept |

---

### 2. UP Eco-Tourism Huts & Tharu Community Stays

- **Dudhwa Eco-Cottages**: Modern wooden cottages managed by UP Eco Tourism adjacent to Dudhwa main gate.
- **Tharu Tribal Heritage Huts**: Authentic indigenous eco-lodges operated in partnership with the local Tharu community (Hathnapur & Rana Tharu villages), featuring traditional art, organic local dining, and handicraft showcases.

### 3. Private Resorts & Eco-Lodges (Verified External Options)

- **Dudhwa Jungle Lore / Forest View Resorts**: Located along the Palia-Dudhwa state highway (buffer border).
- **Jaag Hotel & Heritage Stays**: Boutique eco-retreats near Palia Kalan.
