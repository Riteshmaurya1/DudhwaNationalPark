# IMAGE RIGHTS & LICENSING TAXONOMY
## Dudhwa Digital Experience

### 1. Compliance Principles
1. **No Unlicensed Scraping**: Images must never be blindly scraped from Google Images, Pinterest, or YouTube thumbnails without verifying license parameters.
2. **Explicit Metadata Attribution**: Every image rendered on the website will include visible or accessible hover metadata attributing the photographer and license type.
3. **Usage Categorization**:
   - **USABLE (Open License)**: CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, Public Domain, Wikimedia Commons.
   - **PERMISSION REQUIRED**: Private photographer portfolios requiring written clearance.
   - **REFERENCE ONLY**: Internal mockups or structural placeholding; excluded from production builds.

---

### 2. Licensing Audit Table

| Asset Category | Target License Type | Verification Method | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Official Reserve Imagery** | UP Government / Forest Dept | Official Ecotourism Press Kit | Retain official credit notice `© UP Forest Department`. |
| **Community & Wildlife Photos** | Creative Commons (CC-BY 4.0) | Wikimedia / Flickr Commons API filter | Include author link & CC license indicator. |
| **Photographer Submissions** | Direct Permission | Written email / waiver form | Store permission record in asset metadata. |

---

### 3. Takedown Protocol
A lightweight contact mechanism will exist in the site footer enabling wildlife photographers to submit attribution updates or license verification queries directly to the editorial team.
