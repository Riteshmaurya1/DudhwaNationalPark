# CONSERVATION INITIATIVES & ECOLOGICAL CHALLENGES
## Dudhwa Tiger Reserve Landscape

### 1. Key Conservation Programs

#### Rhino Rehabilitation Project (Kakraha RRA)
- **Objective**: Re-establish a viable wild population of Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros in the Terai floodplains.
- **Enclosure Structure**: 27 sq km solar-electrified fence in South Sonaripur range.
- **Milestones**: Successful breeding over 4 decades; population increased from 9 founding individuals to ~45+ today.
- **Phase 2 Expansion**: Creation of a second 14 sq km rehabilitation enclosure in Belrayan range (Bhadi area) to disperse genetic diversity and reduce territorial overcrowding.

#### Grassland & Phanta Management
- **Controlled Burning**: Prescribed winter burning of tall thatch grasses (*Imperata cylindrica*, *Saccharum*) to generate fresh green shoots for Barasingha and wild herbivores.
- **Desiltation & Wetland Revival**: De-silting of forest waterbodies (e.g. Jhadi Tal, Kakraha tal, Bankatti ponds) to sustain water availability through hot summer months.

---

### 2. Ecological Challenges & Threats

1. **Linear Infrastructure & Connectivity**:
   - Railway line passing through core Dudhwa forest (Palia-Gola track) causing animal strikes.
   - Heavy vehicular traffic along state highways bordering buffer ranges.
2. **Indo-Nepal Corridor Fragmentation**:
   - Human settlement expansion along the international border restricts wildlife movement between Dudhwa and Suklaphanta / Bardia National Parks in Nepal.
3. **Human-Tiger & Human-Elephant Conflict**:
   - Sugarcane fields in surrounding agricultural villages act as pseudo-forest cover, leading to big cats straying outside core forest boundaries.
   - Rapid response teams and village tiger guards (*Bagh Mitra*) trained by UP Forest Dept & WWF.
