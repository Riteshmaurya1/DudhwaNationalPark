### Secondary Scientific & Conservation Partners

1. **Wildlife Institute of India (WII)**: Census methodology, tiger landscape connectivity studies, Terai Arc Landscape (TAL) reports.
2. **WWF India**: Rhino reintroduction monitoring, camera trap studies, human-wildlife conflict mitigation data in South Sonaripur and Kakraha.
3. **IUCN Red List of Threatened Species**: Conservation status taxonomy (*Rucervus duvaucelii duvaucelii* - Vulnerable/Endangered, *Panthera tigris* - Endangered, *Rhinoceros unicornis* - Vulnerable).
