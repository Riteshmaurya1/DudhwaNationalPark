# VERIFIED WILDLIFE SPECIES COMPENDIUM
## Dudhwa National Park & Terai Ecosystem

### 1. Key Flagship Mammals

#### Royal Bengal Tiger (*Panthera tigris*)
- **Status**: Endangered (IUCN Red List)
- **Habitat in Dudhwa**: Sal forests, dense riverine tracts along Suheli and Mohana rivers, tall grassland phantas.
- **Ecological Role**: Apex predator of the Terai landscape. Dudhwa Tiger Reserve hosts an estimated 100–125+ tigers across its three core sectors (Dudhwa NP, Kishanpur, Katarniaghat).

#### Great One-Horned Rhinoceros (*Rhinoceros unicornis*)
- **Status**: Vulnerable (IUCN Red List)
- **Habitat in Dudhwa**: Kakraha Rhino Rehabilitation Area (RRA) in South Sonaripur range (~27 sq km fenced sanctuary).
- **History**: Reintroduced in 1984 with 5 rhinos from Assam (Pobitora) and 4 from Nepal (Chitwan, 1985). Current population: ~45+ individuals. Phase 2 expansion includes additional soft enclosure zones in Belrayan range.

#### Northern Swamp Deer / Barasingha (*Rucervus duvaucelii duvaucelii*)
- **Status**: Vulnerable / Endangered subspecies
- **Habitat in Dudhwa**: Wet tall grasslands (**Phantas**) at Sathiana, Sonaripur, and Jhadi Tal (Kishanpur).
- **Features**: Distinguishing 12-tined antlers in mature stags. Nominate subspecies endemic to the marshy Terai. Population in DTR exceeds 3,500 stags and hinds.

#### Asian Elephant (*Elephas maximus*)
- **Status**: Endangered
- **Habitat in Dudhwa**: Seasonal migration corridors between Nepal (Suklaphanta/Bardia) and Dudhwa through the Mohana river corridors. Resident herds exist in Katarniaghat and Belrayan.

#### Indian Leopard (*Panthera pardus*)
- **Status**: Vulnerable
- **Habitat in Dudhwa**: Forest fringes, sugarcane fields along buffer zones, dense woodland canopy. High density in Katarniaghat and Kishanpur.

#### Hispid Hare (*Caprolagus hispidus*)
- **Status**: Endangered
- **Habitat**: Extremely rare endemic herbivore of the Terai tall thatch grasslands. Rediscovered in Dudhwa's protected phantas.

### 2. Avian Diversity (Over 450+ Species)

- **Bengal Florican (*Houbaropsis bengalensis*)**: Critically Endangered bustard species dependent on pristine wet grasslands.
- **Sarus Crane (*Antigone antigone*)**: Vulnerable, world's tallest flying bird, frequenting wetlands along Suheli river.
- **Swamp Francolin (*Ortygornis gularis*)**: Wetland endemic bird species abundant in Sathiana.
- **Raptors**: Pallas's Fish Eagle, Changeable Hawk-Eagle, Crested Serpent Eagle, Osprey.

---

### 3. Herpetofauna & Aquatic Fauna

- **Mugger Crocodile (*Crocodylus palustris*)**: Suheli, Mohana, and Girwa rivers.
- **Gharial (*Gavialis gangeticus*)**: Critically Endangered long-snouted crocodilian with sanctuary breeding population in Katarniaghat (Girwa River).
- **Gangetic River Dolphin (*Platanista gangetica*)**: Endangered freshwater mammal residing in the deep reaches of the Girwa and Sharda rivers.
