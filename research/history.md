# HISTORICAL CHRONOLOGY & CONSERVATION LEGACY
## Dudhwa National Park & Terai Landscape

### 1. Timeline of Protected Status

```
1958  ───  Sonaripur Wildlife Sanctuary established to protect swamp deer (~212 sq km)
1965  ───  Sanctuary expanded to encompass surrounding Sal forests
1977  ───  Upgraded to Dudhwa National Park (490 sq km core) via lobbying of Billy Arjan Singh
1984  ───  Rhinoceros Reintroduction Program launched (5 rhinos brought from Assam)
1985  ───  4 female rhinos translocated from Chitwan, Nepal to Kakraha RRA
1987  ───  Dudhwa Tiger Reserve created under Project Tiger (incorporating Kishanpur Sanctuary)
2000  ───  Katarniaghat Wildlife Sanctuary added to Dudhwa Tiger Reserve umbrella
```

### 2. The Legacy of Kunwar "Billy" Arjan Singh (1917 – 2010)

- **Biography**: Born into the royal family of Kapurthala, Billy Arjan Singh settled near Palia Kalan on his farm named **"Tiger Haven"** bordering the Neora River and Dudhwa forest.
- **Hunting to Conservation**: Abandoned hunting to become one of India's most passionate big cat conservationists.
- **Tiger Haven Experiments**: Hand-reared leopards (*Prince*, *Harriet*) and a zoo-born tiger cub (*Tara*, brought from Twycross Zoo, UK in 1976) and reintroduced them into the Dudhwa forests.
- **Advocacy**: Personally appealed to Prime Minister Indira Gandhi in the mid-1970s to save the swamp deer from sugar mill encroachments and agricultural land clearance, directly resulting in the declaration of Dudhwa National Park in 1977.
- **Honors**: Awarded the WWF Gold Medal (1976), Order of the Golden Ark (1997), and Padma Bhushan (2006).

### 3. Indigenous Tharu Community Connection

- **Heritage**: The **Tharu tribe** has inhabited the dense Terai forests along the Indo-Nepal border for centuries.
- **Ethnobotany & Coexistence**: Historically relied on sustainable forest produce, thatch grass harvest, and fishing.
- **Eco-Tourism Integration**: Modern conservation models involve Tharu youth as nature guides, homestay hosts, and cultural ambassadors for eco-tourists.
