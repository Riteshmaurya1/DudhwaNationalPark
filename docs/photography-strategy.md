# PHOTOGRAPHY STRATEGY & CURATION GUIDELINES
## Editorial Imagery Standard for Dudhwa National Park

### 1. Visual Style & Aesthetic Pillars
- **Cinematic Lighting**: High-contrast golden hour sunlight filtering through Sal forest canopies, mist-shrouded grassland phantas at dawn, dramatic river reflections.
- **Natural Habitat Context**: Animals depicted in their authentic Terai surroundings (Barasingha in wet marshland, Rhinos in tall thatch grass, Tigers along sandy riverbeds of the Suheli).
- **Zero Heavy Filters**: Avoid artificial HDR, neon color saturation, or unnatural airbrushing. Preserve authentic raw textures.

---

### 2. Photography Curation Rules
1. **Authentic Dudhwa Provenance**: Only use photographs originating from Dudhwa National Park, Kishanpur, or Katarniaghat.
2. **Prohibited Generic Stock**: Never insert generic captive tiger photos or tigers from Central Indian reserves (Kanha, Bandhavgarh, Ranthambore) as representative Dudhwa imagery.
3. **Responsive Image Delivery**: All photography must be delivered via `next/image` with WebP/AVIF format optimization, dynamic blur-up placeholders, and explicit `sizes` attributes.
