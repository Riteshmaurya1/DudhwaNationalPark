# DESIGN SYSTEM & BRAND GUIDELINES
## Dudhwa National Park Visual Identity

### 1. Aesthetic Identity Statement
The visual language of Dudhwa is **Cinematic, Editorial, Authentic, and Wild**. It deliberately avoids generic tourism tropes, childish illustrations, and cheap glowing gradients. The interface feels like a high-end natural history journal combined with an interactive museum exhibit.

---

### 2. Color Hierarchy Matrix

```
[ FOUNDATION ]
Pure Black (#050505)  ─── Base background for cinematic dark sections
Near Black (#111111)  ─── Secondary background for cards & structural containers
Pure White (#FFFFFF)  ─── Crisp canvas for light editorial reading
Soft White (#F5F5F0)  ─── Muted background for paper-like editorial sections

[ BRAND ACCENT ]
Primary Orange (#F97316) ─── Primary CTA buttons, brand badges, section highlights
Deep Orange (#EA580C)    ─── Active click state & primary hover accent

[ INTERACTION ACCENT ]
Forest Green (#1F6F4A)  ─── Hover borders, selected filter tags, active nav indicators
Deep Green (#124734)    ─── Deep green backdrop & subtle status badges

[ NEUTRALS ]
Border Gray (#D9D9D2)   ─── Subtle 1px structural grid lines
Muted Gray (#777777)    ─── Body metadata, captions, scientific names
```

---

### 3. Surface & Card System
- **Default Card State**: Near Black (`#111111`) background with a subtle 1px border (`#222222` or `#D9D9D2` on light mode).
- **Hover Interaction**: Card border smoothly transitions to **Forest Green (`#1F6F4A`)** with a subtle 4px vertical rise (`translateY(-4px)`).
- **CTA Rule**: Primary actions use **Orange (`#F97316`)** fill with white/black text. Secondary actions use subtle ghost borders.
